# Controlled Data Sources

The source register is embedded in `assets/data/manufacturer_sources.json` and the Rev2 engineering master workbook.

## MV cable and transformer sources

Initial cable manufacturers include Tenaga Cable Industries, Leader Cable, Southern Cable, Tai Sin, Federal Power and Universal Cable. Initial transformer manufacturers include Malaysia Transformer Manufacturing, EWT Transformer, Schneider Electric and WEG.

## Protection and switchgear sources

Family-level references include:

- Schneider Electric: EvoPacT, MCSeT, RM6 and MasterPact MTZ
- ABB: VD4 and SACE Emax 2
- Siemens: SION and SENTRON 3WA
- Eaton: IZMX / INX

Manufacturer-family availability is distinct from a selected product model. Exact voltage, current, breaking duty, Icu/Ics/Icw, control voltage, accessories, interlocks and trip-unit ranges must be verified from the current local catalogue and quotation.

## Standards and requirements

Applicable reference groups include IEC 60502-2, IEC 60287, IEC 60949, IEC 60076, IEC 60909, IEC 61936-1, IEC 62271, IEC 60282-1, IEC 60947-2, IEC 61869, IEC 60255, Malaysian Standards, Suruhanjaya Tenaga legislation, TNB ESAH and supplementary IEEE guidance.

The app uses standards only as an engineering framework. It does not reproduce restricted standards tables or replace licensed documents, project specifications, utility requirements or competent-person verification.
